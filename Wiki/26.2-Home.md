## 🌟 Parity Highlights in 26.2

1. **Mixin Modernization**: Includes MixinExtras `@WrapOperation` in `CrossbowItemMixin` on `performShooting`, wrapping `shootProjectile` directly for maximum injection stability.
2. **True Sandbox Freedom**: Removed artificial numeric bounds across all crossbow GameRules in accordance with the *Player Agency & Anti-Nanny Invariant*, unlocking values up to `Integer.MAX_VALUE`.
3. **Dedicated Server Hardening**: Completely isolated singleplayer cap resolution behind `@Environment(EnvType.CLIENT)` helpers, guaranteeing crash-free execution on headless Linux servers.
4. **Dynamic Creative Tab Updates**: Instant invalidation of `CACHED_PARAMETERS` in `CreativeModeTabs` when GameRules change.
