## 🌟 What's New in 26.3

1. **Mixin Modernization**: Adopted MixinExtras `@WrapOperation` in `CrossbowItemMixin` for `performShooting`, wrapping `shootProjectile` directly and eliminating fragile ordinal injections.
2. **True Sandbox Freedom**: Removed artificial numeric bounds across all crossbow GameRules in accordance with the *Player Agency & Anti-Nanny Invariant*, allowing values up to `Integer.MAX_VALUE`.
3. **Dedicated Server Hardening**: Completely isolated singleplayer cap resolution behind `@Environment(EnvType.CLIENT)` helpers, guaranteeing crash-free execution on headless Linux servers.
4. **Instant Creative Tab Cache Flushing**: Live GameRule changes invalidate `CACHED_PARAMETERS` in `CreativeModeTabs`, updating enchanted books instantly without world reloads.
